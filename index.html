FAQ

Questions
answered.

Everything you need to know before getting started. Still have a question? Just drop us a message.

Our Starter package is €349 once off — up to 4 pages, live in 5 days. Our Professional package is €599 once off — up to 8 pages, live in 7 days, with a free month of the care plan included. There are no hidden extras. What you see is what you pay.
Not unless you want to. The once-off fee covers design, build and launch. The only ongoing costs are your domain (usually €15–30/year, which you buy yourself) and optionally our Monthly Care Plan at €30/month if you'd like us to handle updates. The care plan is completely optional and can be cancelled anytime.
We handle all hosting on your behalf — no setup required on your end. Your site is hosted on fast, reliable infrastructure at no extra charge. You just buy your domain (we'll point you to the right place) and we take care of everything else.
Starter sites are typically live in 5 days, Professional sites in 7 days. Once we have your content (logo, photos, text) and any feedback on the preview, we move fast. Most clients are up and running within a week of first contact.
We don't use WordPress or any CMS, so there's no login for you to edit the site directly. Instead, our Monthly Care Plan (€30/month) covers up to 3 changes per month turned around within 48 hours — just message us. Most small businesses find this far less hassle than managing a CMS themselves.
We specialise in professional brochure websites for small businesses — the kind that showcase your services and get customers to call or enquire. We don't build ecommerce stores with payment processing. If you need a full online shop, we'd recommend Shopify for that.
We don't build custom booking systems, but we integrate beautifully with free tools like Calendly. We add a booking button to your site that links directly to your Calendly page — clean, simple, and free. Most clients love this approach.
Every site we build includes SEO basics — proper page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, fast loading speeds and mobile optimisation. This gives you a solid foundation. We don't offer ongoing SEO campaigns, but getting these fundamentals right from day one makes a real difference.
We use AI tools to build faster and keep costs low — the same way any modern developer would. Every site is custom designed for your business, reviewed and refined by a real person, and built to a high standard. That's how we can charge €349 instead of €3,000.
Not much — just a quick chat about your business, your logo if you have one, any photos you'd like to use, and the text for each page. No text? No problem. We can write it for you based on what you tell us. We'll guide you through every step.
