FAQ

Questions answered.

Still have a question? Ask us anything

How much does a website cost?
Our Starter package is €299 once off: up to 5 pages, live in 5 days. Our Professional package is €499 once off: up to 10 pages, live in 7 days, with a free month of the care plan. No hidden extras.
How do I pay?
Securely through Stripe, once you're happy with your preview. Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your card details go straight to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us, and you get a receipt by email.
Are there any ongoing costs?
Only your domain, usually €15–30 a year, which you buy in your own name. Updates are optional: our Care Plan is €30 a month for up to 3 changes, or pay €19.99 for a one-off update whenever you need one.
Can I update the site myself?
There's no login to edit it yourself, so there's nothing for you to learn or break. Just message us with what you'd like changed and it's done within 48 hours on the Care Plan, or as a €19.99 one-off.
Who owns my domain and hosting?
You own your domain, bought under your own account, so it's always yours. We host your site for you on fast, reliable infrastructure at no extra charge and simply point your domain to it.
How long does it take to go live?
Starter sites are typically live in 5 days and Professional sites in 7 days, once we have your content and your feedback on the preview.
Will my site show up on Google?
Every site includes SEO basics: page titles, descriptions, proper headings, fast loading and mobile optimisation. We don't run ongoing SEO campaigns, but these fundamentals make a real difference.
Can you add bookings or an online shop?
Yes to bookings: we add a button that links to free tools like Calendly. We don't build full online shops with checkout; for that we'd recommend Shopify.
Are these AI-generated websites?
We use AI tools to build faster and keep costs low, like any modern developer. Every site is custom designed for your business and reviewed by a real person. That's how we charge €299, not €3,000.
What do you need from me to get started?
A quick chat about your business, your logo if you have one, some photos, and the text for each page. No text? We'll write it from what you tell us.
