Thinking about a .ie? It takes a few more steps

A .com is quicker to set up and works just as well for Irish customers. Most of our clients choose one.

  • Namecheap doesn't sell .ie, so buy your .ie on GoDaddy instead.
  • You'll need to prove an Irish connection, such as a CRO number, Revenue registration or ID with an Irish address.
  • The .ie registry checks this before your domain goes live, so it can take up to a day to be approved.
  • Once it's approved, follow the GoDaddy steps below to give us access, same as any other domain.

At checkout, do

  • Buy just the domain, for 1 year (or longer if you like)
  • Leave auto-renew on, so you never lose your address
  • Use your own email and card, so it's fully yours

Skip the extras

  • Email or Microsoft 365 plans
  • Website builders or hosting (we host your site for you)
  • SSL certificates, "protection" or security add-ons

Most domains cost about €15–30 a year. The renewal price can be higher than the first year, and that's normal.
