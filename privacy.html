Privacy notice

Last updated 19 November 2026

This explains what personal information Rebel Web collects, why, and your rights. We keep it simple because we collect very little.

Who we are

Rebel Web is a web design service based in Cork, Ireland. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact: hello@rebelweb.ie.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us by email, WhatsApp or Instagram: your name, contact details and anything you tell us about your business and project.
  • Project materials you give us for your website, such as your logo, photos, text and business details.
  • Payment and invoice records when you become a client. Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.

This website has no contact form, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. It does not set cookies.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry, give you a quote and build your website. This is to take steps at your request and to carry out our contract with you.
  • To invoice you and keep accounting records. We must do this to meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • To run and improve our service, for example showing finished work in our portfolio (with your agreement) and handling support. This is our legitimate interest.

Who we share it with

We only share what is needed, with services that help us run the business. We do not sell your information.

  • Stripe, for payments and receipts.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub, which host and store the websites we build.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram (Meta) and our email provider, when you contact us through them.
  • Google Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google, so Google may receive your IP address when you visit.

Some of these providers are based outside the EU. Where they are, they rely on safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client project files and messages: for as long as we work together, then for a reasonable period afterwards in case you need further changes.
  • Invoices and payment records: 6 years, as Irish tax rules require.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, to move it to another provider, and to object to how we use it. Email hello@rebelweb.ie and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top.
